Just like the real thing

What shall we squish today?

Pour paint from a bottle, mix it with your finger, then stretch, squeeze, poke and fold. The slime flows, stretches and slowly settles back like a real material.

  1. 1 Pour

    Pick a bottle from the shelf and let the paint flow into the bowl. Tilt the phone to pour more.

  2. 2 Mix

    Drag your finger through it. The colours marble first, then blend into one as you keep going.

  3. 3 Stretch

    In the Play Area, pull it with two fingers, squeeze, poke and fold. Every touch has its own sound and haptic feel.

Blue and yellow make green.

Here colours mix like real paint, not like light on a screen: blue and yellow turn green, not grey. Under the hood is a model from paint science (Kubelka–Munk).

Free: 27 colours, 4 textures (Glossy, Cloud, Butter, Clear) and 5 add-ins. Put a slime you love in a jar and tell a friend its recipe code so they can make the same one.

8 mini games

Take a little breather

Each game has one toy, a few options and its own sounds. You always play by touching the object itself.

  • {{pop_it}}

    Square, heart, round, cloud; 4 colour schemes, 3 sizes.

  • Stress Magnet

    A pair of hematite magnets; flick, shake, toss up. “Zzzrt.”

  • {{kinetic_sand}}

    A turning bar smooths the sand; you mess it up, it fills it back in.

  • Bubble Wrap

    An endless roll in three sizes; every bubble crackles differently.

  • Cutting Board

    Real-looking soap bars and sponges; you pull the knife yourself.

  • Zen Garden

    Rake the sand, place stones, draw waves.

  • Breathing Balloon

    Breathe in as the balloon grows, out as it shrinks. 4-7-8.

  • Water Ripples

    Tap to drop a drip; watch the rings spread and the sound fade.
