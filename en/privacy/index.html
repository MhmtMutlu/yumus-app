Yumuş Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Yumuş is an ad-free slime and relaxation game made for children and families.

In short

  • We collect no personal data at all.
  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Your settings and jars stay on your device.
  • Purchases go through Apple and are protected by a parental question.

Data we collect

None. Yumuş does not ask you to create an account and never asks for your name, email address, age, location or device identifier. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

Your settings (such as sound, haptics and the break reminder) and the slime jars you save are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them too.

If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, these files may be included in Apple's backup; we cannot see those backups. The player profile (age range) chosen at first launch is never saved.

Photos

In “Pop It from a photo”, the photo you choose is opened with Apple's photo picker; Yumuş does not ask for access to your photo library. The photo is turned into a small grid of colours on your device; the photo itself is not stored or shared. Only colours reach the board, never people.

Motion sensors

Some games use the device's motion sensors to notice tilting and shaking. This information is processed on the device, only while you play, and is not stored.

Purchases

Yumuş Plus is a one-time purchase made through Apple's App Store and is protected by a parental question. When you buy or restore it, Apple's own system makes the connection. We never see your payment details.

Crash reports

If “Share With App Developers” is turned on in your device settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. This choice is controlled by Apple and can be turned off at any time; Yumuş itself does not send reports.

Children's privacy

Yumuş does not collect personal data from children (COPPA, KVKK and the provisions of the GDPR concerning children). There is no chat or share sheet in the app. The only outbound link, Apple's standard licence terms on the purchase screen, sits behind the parental question; a recipe code only carries colour and texture information. This policy can also be read inside the app, under Settings → Privacy.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or tracking and loads nothing from third-party servers; its fonts and images are served by the site itself. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; to run the service, GitHub may keep technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new text on this page and update the date above.
