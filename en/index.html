Realistic physics

Pour, mix, knead

The slime is driven by a real-time material simulation: it flows, stretches, keeps a dent for a while and slowly settles back. Every touch answers with its own sound and haptic feel.

  1. 1 Pour

    Choose a colour from the shelf and let the paint flow into the bowl. Tilt the phone to pour faster.

  2. 2 Mix

    Drag your finger through it: the colours marble first, then blend into a single shade as you keep mixing.

  3. 3 Play

    In the Play Area, stretch the slime with two fingers, squeeze it, poke it and fold it.

Blue and yellow make green.

Colours mix like real pigments rather than light on a screen: blue and yellow turn green, not grey. A spectral mixing model from paint science makes this possible.

The free version includes 27 colours, 4 textures (Glossy, Cloud, Butter, Clear) and 5 add-ins. Save a slime you love in a jar and share its recipe code so friends can make the same one.

Mini games

Eight calming toys

Each is built around a single object: few options, direct touch and sounds of its own.

  • Pop It

    Square, heart, round and cloud shapes; four colour schemes, three sizes.

  • Stress Magnet

    A pair of hematite magnets: flick them, shake them, toss them up; they buzz as they snap together.

  • Sand Tray

    Draw patterns in the sand; a turning bar smooths the surface again.

  • Bubble Wrap

    An endless roll in three sizes; every bubble pops in its own way.

  • Cutting Board

    Slice soap bars and sponges with a knife; every slice tips over realistically.

  • Zen Garden

    Rake lines into the sand, place stones and draw waves.

  • Breathing Balloon

    Breathe in as the balloon grows and out as it shrinks; 4-7-8 and box breathing.

  • Water Ripples

    Tap to let a drop fall and watch the rings spread and blend across the water.

No scores, no timers, no ads. Just play.

For parents

Hand it over with peace of mind

Yumuş is designed to help children wind down, not to keep them on the screen. It contains no ads, no tracking and no unexpected payments.

  • No ads

    No banners, pop-ups or rewarded videos. No third-party advertising or tracking software is used.

  • No subscriptions

    The one-time Yumuş Plus is the only purchase; there are no virtual currencies, energy systems or limited-time offers.

  • No data collected

    No accounts, servers or analytics. Settings and saved slimes are stored only on the device. Privacy policy

  • Parental gate

    Purchases are protected by a multiplication question for an adult, followed by the App Store’s own approval. The only external link, to the licence terms, sits behind this question.

  • Break reminder

    By default, a calm break screen appears every 20 minutes. It can be set to 40 minutes or turned off in Settings.

  • Family Sharing

    Yumuş Plus is bought once; with Family Sharing, everyone in the family group can use it.

  • No internet required

    No account or internet connection is needed. The App Store is contacted only when buying or restoring Yumuş Plus.
